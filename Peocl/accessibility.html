Accessibility statement

Peocl Ltd. wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who browse with a keyboard, a screen reader, magnification, or other assistive technology.

Conformance status

This website (peocl.com) has been built and reviewed against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 (opens in a new tab) at level AA, which is also the level required by Israeli Standard 5568. We believe the site meets that level, with the exceptions listed under “Known limitations” below.

What we have done

  • Every page can be operated with the keyboard alone, in a logical order, with a clearly visible focus ring.
  • A “Skip to main content” link is the first thing a keyboard or screen-reader user reaches.
  • Headings, landmarks, lists, and link text are marked up so assistive technology can navigate the structure.
  • Text and interface colours were measured to meet the AA contrast ratios (4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and interface elements).
  • Decorative icons and illustrations are hidden from screen readers so they don't add noise; meaningful images and videos have text alternatives.
  • Animation respects the operating-system “reduce motion” setting, and can also be switched off from the accessibility menu.
  • The page reflows to a single column and stays usable when text is enlarged or the window is narrow.

The accessibility menu

The button at the bottom corner of every page (the accessibility icon) opens a menu with these options, which are remembered on your next visit:

  • Text size — enlarge the text up to 150%.
  • High contrast — black background, white text, yellow links.
  • Grayscale — removes colour.
  • Highlight links — underlines and outlines every link.
  • Readable font — switches to a plain, evenly spaced typeface.
  • Stop animations — freezes motion and auto-playing video.
  • Large cursor — an enlarged mouse pointer.
  • Reset — returns everything to the default.

The menu opens and closes with the keyboard (Enter or Space to open, Escape to close), and each option reports its on/off state to screen readers.

Known limitations

We are aware of the following, and would rather say so than imply otherwise:

  • Sample video clips. The wave clips on the home page are silent footage — they contain no speech and no audio information — so they carry a text description but no captions or audio description.
  • PDF documents. The privacy policy and terms are served as PDF files that have not been remediated for full screen-reader accessibility. If you need either document in an accessible format, contact us and we will send it to you as plain text or HTML.
  • Third-party destinations. Pages we link to but do not control — the Apple App Store, Google Play, our partners' websites, and payment providers — have their own accessibility levels.
  • Our apps. This statement covers this website. The Peocl guest apps (iOS, Android, and the web apps for WaterCap and Surfai) are separate products and are not covered here.

Found a problem? Tell us

If you run into a page, control, or document you cannot use, please tell us what you were trying to do and what got in the way — including the page address and the assistive technology you use, if relevant. We treat these reports as bugs and will get back to you.
