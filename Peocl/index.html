Advanced park photography systems

Every ride. Every wave.
Captured, matched to the guest, and sold.

Peocl builds automatic photo & video capture systems for action parks — water parks and surf parks. Our cameras identify the guest's wristband, record the moment, and our apps deliver and sell the media the same day.

What we do

🎢

On-slide capture

Camera rigs on water slides detect the rider's RFID wristband and record a full video of the run plus action photos — automatically, ride after ride.

🏄

Wave & surf capture

Session filming for artificial wave lagoons: per-surfer footage of every wave, matched to the surfer, ready to review technique or share the highlight.

📷

Park photographers

Pro photographers with Canon gear roam the park; every shot is matched to the guest's wristband and appears in their app within minutes.

📱

Guest apps & payments

iOS, Android, and web apps where guests link their wristband, preview everything free, and unlock packages with secure in-app payment (Tranzila, PCI-DSS).
