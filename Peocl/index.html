Advanced park photography systems

Every ride. Every wave.
Captured, matched to the guest, and sold.

Peocl builds photo & video capture systems for action parks — water parks and surf parks. The guest scans their wristband at the ride, our cameras record the moment, and our apps deliver and sell the media the same day.

What we do

🎢

On-slide capture

The rider scans their RFID wristband at the slide station, and our camera rigs record a full video of the run plus action photos — ride after ride.

🏄

Wave & surf capture

Session filming for artificial wave lagoons: per-surfer footage of every wave, matched to the surfer, ready to review technique or share the highlight.

📷

Park photographers

Pro photographers roam the park; every shot is matched to the guest's wristband and appears in their app within minutes.

📱

Guest apps & payments

iOS, Android, and web apps where guests link their wristband, preview everything free, and unlock packages with secure in-app payment (Tranzila, PCI-DSS).

Sample footage

Straight from our wave capture system at SRF Park TLV: every wave a surfer catches is filmed, auto-clipped, and matched to their session — these are real, unedited clips exactly as guests receive them in the Surfai app.
