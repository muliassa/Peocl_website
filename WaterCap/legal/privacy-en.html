WaterCap — Privacy Policy

Your waterpark memories, captured and delivered to you
Effective date: July 6, 2026  ·  Version: 1.1  ·  Applies to the WaterCap iOS app, Android app, and web app

WaterCap (the “Service”) is operated by Peocl Ltd. (“we”, “us”, “our”). The Service lets waterpark guests link their park wristband to their own device and then view and purchase photos and videos captured of them at the park — on water slides and by park photographers. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using the Service you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Device and registration data

  • When you first use the Service, your device is registered anonymously with our cloud service and receives a random client identifier and access token. No account, name, email address, or phone number is required.
  • We collect basic technical details: device model or browser type, operating system and version, and app version. This is used to operate and secure the Service.
  • If you enable notifications, we store a push notification token for your device.

1.2 Wristband and scan data

  • When you link a wristband at a park scanner, we associate the wristband identifier with your device’s client identifier, together with the scanner number, date, and time of the link.
  • The wristband identifier is a technical code; it does not itself contain personal details.
  • Linked wristband identifiers are also stored locally on your device (or in your browser’s local storage) so your wristbands remain available when you return to the app.

1.3 Photos and videos

  • Cameras installed on selected water slides and park photographers capture photos and video clips of guests during their visit. Each item is associated with the wristband detected at the ride or photo point.
  • These media items may show you and people with you, and are personal information. They are made available only to devices that have linked that wristband via a park scanner.
  • Signage at the park marks areas where cameras operate. If you do not wish to be photographed, please avoid marked attractions or contact park staff.
  • Capture is not guaranteed. We do not commit that any particular ride, slide, moment, or visit will be photographed or recorded. Coverage depends on camera positions, wristband detection, lighting, and technical conditions, and some or all media for a visit may not exist. A purchase unlocks only the media actually captured for your wristband.

1.4 Purchases and payment

  • Payments are processed by Tranzila, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processor. Card and Bit payment details are entered directly into Tranzila’s secure fields and never reach or get stored on our servers.
  • We receive only payment confirmation details: transaction reference, amount, date, and the wristband the purchase unlocks.

1.5 Service logs

  • Like most online services, our servers keep technical logs (IP address, request time, requested resource) used for security, troubleshooting, and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the Service: link wristbands, and show and deliver the media captured for your wristband.
  • To process purchases and unlock purchased media.
  • To secure the Service, prevent fraud and abuse, and enforce the per-scanner linking process.
  • To provide support and resolve technical problems.
  • To comply with legal obligations (for example, tax record-keeping for purchases).

We do not use your photos or videos for advertising, do not sell personal information, and do not use the media for automated face recognition beyond associating media with the wristband detected at the ride.

3. Who Can See Your Media

  • Media associated with a wristband is accessible to any device that has physically linked that wristband at a park scanner. Keep your wristband safe and do not allow others to scan it.
  • Before purchase, reduced-quality previews are shown; full-quality media is unlocked after purchase.
  • Park staff with an operational need (for example, the park photographer team) may access media as part of operating the Service.

4. Sharing of Information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Service, and only to the extent needed:

  • Tranzila — payment processing.
  • Hosting and content-delivery providers — storing and delivering media and operating our cloud service.
  • The waterpark operator — for operating cameras, scanners, and guest support at the park.

We may also disclose information if required by law, court order, or a competent authority, or to protect the rights and safety of guests, the park, or the Service. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Retention

  • Photos and videos, and wristband-to-device links, are kept for a limited period after the visit day — currently 7 days — and are then permanently deleted from our systems. Please view, purchase, and download your media within this period; deleted media cannot be recovered.
  • Purchase records are kept as required by Israeli tax and accounting law (up to 7 years), without payment card details.
  • Technical logs are kept for a short period for security purposes.
  • You may remove a wristband from your device at any time in the app; this removes it from your device’s stored list.

6. Security

  • All communication between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Media access requires knowledge of the linked wristband identifier obtained through the physical scanner linking process.
  • Payments are handled exclusively by Tranzila under PCI-DSS Level 1.
  • No system is perfectly secure; please report any concern to the contact below.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Service is intended for use by adults. Parents and guardians may link wristbands worn by their children to view and purchase media of them. Media of minors is subject to the same access controls as all other media: it is visible only to devices that physically linked the wristband. If you believe media of your child is accessible to someone without authority, contact us immediately and we will remove it.

8. Your Rights

Under the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, and where applicable other privacy laws (such as the EU GDPR for visitors from the EU), you have the right to:

  • Request access to the information we hold that relates to you;
  • Request correction or deletion of that information (including deletion of photos and videos of you);
  • Withdraw consent to future capture by avoiding marked attractions.

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. We may ask you to prove the connection between you, your device, and the wristband in question.

9. Cookies and Local Storage (Web App)

The web app does not use advertising or analytics cookies. It uses the browser’s local storage only to keep your anonymous device registration and your linked wristbands so they persist between visits. Clearing your browser’s site data removes them.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version, with its effective date, is always available in the apps and on the Service website. Material changes will be announced in the app.
